What this site does and does not do

This site is a marketing site. You can read it, subscribe to a newsletter by typing your email, talk to an AI chat assistant, and fill in any enquiry form that is live on the site at the time. There is no account for you to create, nothing for you to log into, and no payment is taken through this site.

The chat assistant is an AI. It can describe our services, but nothing it says is a binding offer, a price quote, or professional advice. Quotes come from us in writing, after we have talked.

What you type to it goes to our own agent service at cloudyjoe.com, along with which page you were reading, and from there to a language-model provider to produce the reply.

It also offers a voice mode, which only runs if you switch it on. Turning it on sends your microphone audio to Google’s Gemini Live API for a spoken reply, forwards the recent part of your typed conversation for context, and returns a transcript, which is also sent to our agent service, including if you close the tab mid-conversation.

Don’t put anything confidential into it, spoken or typed. It is a sales assistant on a marketing site, not a secure channel. The privacy policy, linked below, is the detailed version.
