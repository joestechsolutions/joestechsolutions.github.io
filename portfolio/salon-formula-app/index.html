The problem.

Hair color is chemistry with a memory problem. A colorist mixes by eye and experience (1:1.5 ratio, 20 volume, 60 grams), and six weeks later the client asks for “the same thing as last time,” and the answer lives in a notebook nobody can find. The owner wanted that ledger on their phone: capture a mix, find it again by name, brand or shade, and never lose it.

The build.

Built for speed. Capture is sliders for ratio and developer volume, short fields for brand, shade and grams, and an optional photo of the bowl on the scale.

Built to fit the workflow. The stack stays out of the way: a phone-native app backed by cloud sync, so formulas survive a lost phone. Feedback from the beta drives what ships next; the feedback loop behind that is its own story.
